About Rainhood
Welcome to the
Hood.

Rainhood is an independent digital collectible project focused on building a recognizable universe of unique characters.

Each character represents a different energy, personality and visual identity inside the Rainhood universe.

The project is being built on Robinhood Chain, an Ethereum-compatible Layer 2 network.
